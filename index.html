01 THE BASICS

Know what to look for.

Anyone can be a target. Knowing the signs is your first line of defense.

◎
THE BIG PICTURE

What is phishing?

Phishing is when someone pretends to be a trusted person or organization to trick you into sharing information, sending money, or opening a harmful link or file.

↳ It can happen through email, text, social media, or a phone call.
⌁
THE TACTICS

Common techniques

  • Fake account or password alerts
  • Unexpected delivery or payment notices
  • Impersonating a boss, bank, or friend
  • Too-good-to-be-true prizes or offers
!
THE WARNING SIGNS

Red flags to watch for

  • Pressure to act immediately
  • Unexpected requests for passwords or codes
  • Unfamiliar senders or strange addresses
  • Links that don't match the real organization
✓
THE SMART RESPONSE

Protect yourself

  • Pause before opening links or attachments
  • Verify using a known website or phone number
  • Report suspicious messages, then delete them
  • Use unique passwords and multi-factor authentication
✦

Remember: A familiar name or polished logo doesn't guarantee a message is real. When in doubt, verify it another way.
